A short, funny rant about an everyday annoyance — the reply-all email
chain — written for the [Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/c/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046)
on make.fun.

## The entry

> One person replied-all 'thanks!' to a company-wide email. Now four hundred people are replying-all to say please stop replying-all. Every one of them makes it worse — a fire crew arriving with petrol.

## How it works

`main.py` assembles the rant from three beats — the spark, the pile-on,
and the punchline — validates it against the 400-character gate, and
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
